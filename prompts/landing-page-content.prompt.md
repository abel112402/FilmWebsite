# Task: Generate the Kinext Landing Page
Create a single-page landing page for Kinext based on the `kinext-tech-guidelines`. The website language is strictly English.

## Navigation / Menu
- **Position:** Top-left corner hamburger menu.
- **Behavior:** Clicking it opens a side-drawer menu sliding in from the left.
- **Menu Items (Anchor links to sections):** Our vision, The Creative Lab, Kickoff Residency, International mentors, The team, Cooperation.

## Page Sections (Top to Bottom order)

### 1. Hero / Intro Section
- **Layout:** Full-screen (100vh) with a black background.
- **Content:** Large prominent "Kinext" logo.
- **Subtitle:** "Growing the future of central and eastern european cinema."

### 2. Our Vision Section
- **Layout:** Height approx. 50vh.
- **Title:** OUR VISION
- **Text:** Building a stronger cinematic identity for central and eastern europe. We believe Central and Eastern Europe is home to a new generation of filmmakers with distinctive voices and stories that deserve to travel beyond the region. Our vision is to connect these emerging talents, create meaningful creative and professional relationships, and give first films the space, support and connections they need to grow. By bringing together filmmakers, producers and industry professionals from across the region, we aim to create opportunities for collaboration, international co-production and knowledge exchange - while giving Central and Eastern European cinema a stronger and more visible presence on the international stage.

### 3. The Creative Lab Section
- **Layout:** Full-screen (100vh).
- **Title:** GROWING TOGETHER THE CREATIVE LAB
- **Text:** The Creative Lab is where ideas meet people, perspectives and professional experience. Each year, we bring together emerging filmmakers from across Central and Eastern Europe and support them through an intensive development process, from their first concept to a project ready to enter the international film industry. Through script development, creative and producer consultations, mentoring, workshops and international connections, participants are encouraged to challenge their ideas, strengthen their individual voices and build the creative teams around their projects. The goal is not only to develop better films, but to create lasting relationships between the filmmakers shaping the region's future.

### 4. Kick-off Residency Section
- **Layout:** Height approx. 50-75vh.
- **Title:** CREATIVE LAB'S KICK-OFF RESIDENCY
- **Subtitle:** A starting point for collaboration. A first step towards production. A place where regional cinema grows together.
- **Text:** The Creative Lab begins with an intensive week in Hungary, bringing together the selected filmmakers, international mentors and industry professionals from across the region. It is a space to develop ideas, exchange perspectives and discover the people who can help take each project further. Alongside script consultations, participants work on their directing and visual concepts, explore production and financing opportunities, and connect with potential creative and international collaborators. The week culminates in the Greenlight Session, where filmmakers present their projects to producers, financiers, sales agents, distributors, festivals and other industry professionals. From these presentations, five projects are selected to continue into the next stage of intensive development. Applications open in December 2026! Subscribe to our newsletter to stay up-to-date with announcements, deadlines and more:
- **Feature:** Include an email newsletter subscription form (Input field for Email only, and a Submit button).

### 5. International Mentors Section
- **Layout:** Height approx. 50vh.
- **Title:** Our international mentors:
- **Feature:** An interactive carousel displaying mentors (Image, Name, Title side-by-side).

### 6. Our Team Section
- **Layout:** Height approx. 50vh.
- **Style:** Similar layout to the Mentors section but visually distinct (Mentors section should feel more prominent/emphasized). Include Image, Name, and Title.

### 7. Cooperation Section
- **Layout:** Height approx. 25-50vh.
- **Title:** INTERESTED IN COOPERATION?
- **Text:** We are looking for partners, institutions and industry professionals who share our vision for the future of Central and Eastern European cinema. If you are interested in collaborating with us, supporting emerging filmmakers or becoming part of our professional network, we would love to hear from you!
